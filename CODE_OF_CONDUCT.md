# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Accept reasonable feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing viewpoints.
- Help make participation accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary.

Project maintainers are responsible for applying these expectations fairly and consistently.
